## Cancellations and changes

Your cancellation terms come with your payment link, before you pay. Paying
confirms your booking and accepts those terms.

Defaults:

- **Guest Room, Flexible.** Full refund up to 1 day before check-in. Cancel
  inside that day and we charge 1 night. Cancel during your stay and we charge
  1 extra night, then refund the rest.
- **Two Bedroom Unit, Firm.** Full refund at least 30 days before check-in.
  50% refund 7 to 30 days before check-in. After that the booking is
  non-refundable.

The cleaning fee is fully refunded if you cancel before checking in.

We do try to be reasonable. If we can rebook the nights you cancel, we'll
refund those nights.

## Payment

Payment in full confirms your booking and your acceptance of these terms and
conditions. We'll reply to your enquiry with your dates and total, then send a
secure payment link.

## Check-in and check-out

Check-in from 3:00pm, check-out by 10:00am. Early check-in and bag drop are
often possible, just ask.

## Smoking and parties

No smoking or vaping inside, on the decks, or near windows and doors. Our
ventilation system draws fresh air from beside the back deck, so anything
smoked there ends up inside the house. If you need to smoke, please head out to
the street. This applies to your visitors too.

No parties or events, we live here and our neighbours are close.

## Hot tub

The wood-fired hot tub is a paid add-on, not a standard amenity. We heat it to
order, so pricing and availability depend on the day. Ask us when you enquire.
Up to 4 people, swimwear required, please shower before use.

## Pets

Well-behaved medium and large dogs are welcome in the two bedroom unit by
arrangement. The unit has a fenced outdoor space and we can provide a crate if
needed. Small dogs might fit through the fence, so please ask before booking.
